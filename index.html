Why These Options Rank This Way

Routes rank higher when they are verified, simple for kids, close to Guangzhou, and meaningfully cheaper after transfer costs.

1 CAN direct or 1-stop wins when the price is close because it removes final-transfer risk.
2 HKG is a real fallback only when adjusted savings are meaningful and arrival time supports same-day transfer.
3 Points are useful only when all 4 seats are confirmed and value clears the 1.2 cpp floor.
4 Shanghai is a backup, not the default, unless it saves about $400+ after the extra domestic leg.
